Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JustLend DAO

What is JustLend DAO?

JustLend DAO is a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol on the TRON blockchain. It enables users to lend, borrow, stake TRX and TRC20 tokens, swap assets, and earn JST governance rewards.

How to stake TRX on JustLend?

Staking TRX on JustLend is simple: connect your TronLink wallet, go to the 'Staking' section, choose an amount, and confirm. You'll earn staking rewards and JST tokens.

What is the APY for lending?

Lending APY varies by asset. Typically stablecoins offer 2–5% APY, while more volatile assets can yield higher. Check our Markets page for real-time rates.

Can I swap tokens on JustLend?

Yes, JustLend DAO includes a decentralized exchange (DEX) aggregator for swapping TRC20 tokens with low slippage and competitive rates.

Is JustLend safe?

JustLend DAO has been audited by multiple top-tier security firms (CertiK, SlowMist) and has over $1.2 billion in total value locked (TVL).

What is JST token used for?

JST is the governance token of JustLend DAO. Holders can vote on proposals, and it's distributed as rewards for staking and providing liquidity.
